# Five-act main quest

The main quest follows **Mara Venn**, a night-shift mechanic in **Gatorwake**,
whose younger brother disappears after accepting a courier job. She learns that
a ledger hidden in a trap house connects his disappearance to a narcotics
network—and to the first Alien Stalker sightings.

The ledger is her bargaining chip: it identifies shipment routes, paid
intermediaries, and a transfer destination where her brother may still be alive.
Every faction wants it for a different reason.

All characters, organizations, and events in this outline are fictional. Use the
approved names in [the parody dictionary](ParodyTranslationDictionary.md).

## Act I — The House on Ragged Mile

A feud between **Rust Crown Crew** and **Floodline Syndicate** escalates after a
drill track turns a private betrayal into a public humiliation. Threatening
lyrics, retaliatory attacks, and neighborhood rumors make any unfamiliar vehicle
a target.

Mara traces her brother's last delivery to a trap house on **Ragged Mile Road**.
While she searches it, the **Gatorwake Public Order Bureau** launches a raid.
Gang enforcers seal the rear exit, believing someone inside has stolen the ledger.

Then a towering Alien Stalker enters the street. Searchlights swing toward it;
gunfire draws it closer. Mara escapes with the ledger, but the raid footage shows
her leaving the house with evidence.

**Quest objective:** Recover the ledger and survive the first three-way encounter.

## Act II — Northreach Lockdown

**Sheriff Wade Drybanks** imposes emergency checkpoints across **Northreach**.
Mara becomes a suspect, while both crews assume she intends to sell their secrets.

The ledger reveals that the neighborhood feud conceals a larger trafficking
operation run through legitimate-looking freight businesses. **Boglight Boys**
provide local storage and distribution; distant financiers replace lost shipments
and encourage retaliation whenever it disrupts scrutiny.

Mara seeks **Juno Jinx**, a performer whose lyrics contain references to the
courier route. He admits that some lines came from stories supplied by a promoter,
not firsthand knowledge. **Rookie Rook**, his rival, holds a recording that places
Mara's brother alive at a freight depot.

**Quest objective:** Establish proof of life and identify the next transfer point.

**Pressure:** Police watch major roads, enforcers watch familiar contacts, and
Stalkers hunt the noise produced by either side.

## Act III — The Crooked Current Crossing

The transfer route leads across **Crooked Current River**. Mara needs a working
vehicle, fuel, and a passage through a district where neither faction can guarantee
safe travel.

At a riverside warehouse, she discovers that the narcotics network also transported
sealed extraterrestrial objects. Nobody in the street-level crews understood the
cargo. Their employers treated the first disappearances as theft and ordered
reprisals.

The ledger contains locations associated with those objects. Stalkers repeatedly
converge on them. Mara now carries information that could explain the invasion,
while rival crews continue interpreting every movement as a territorial challenge.

She locates her brother, but he refuses an immediate escape: several other captives
will be moved before dawn.

**Quest objective:** Extract the captives and recover the ledger's missing shipment
appendix.

**Act turn:** Mara's goal expands from rescuing one person to preventing the network
from moving the dangerous cargo again.

## Act IV — Crownwalk's Clean Hands

The appendix points to **Crownwalk Commons**, where respectable businesses conceal
the network's financial center. **Brock Bramble**, a public-facing development
broker, has arranged a final shipment while privately negotiating his own evacuation.

Mara must assemble enough corroborating evidence to make the ledger credible:
manifests, witness testimony, and records linking the sealed cargo to the
disappearances. The ledger alone could be dismissed as a fabrication produced
during the feud.

Drybanks offers Mara protection if she surrenders everything. A surviving faction
leader offers transport in exchange for selected pages. Both deals would leave
someone else controlling the truth.

Meanwhile, Stalkers enter Crownwalk. Emergency announcements, alarms, and armed
evacuation convoys turn the district into a moving trap.

**Quest objective:** Secure the evidence and choose who, if anyone, receives an
advance copy.

**Consequence:** Earlier alliances determine which routes remain open and which
witnesses survive to support Mara's account.

## Act V — The Last Shipment

The final convoy gathers at a river freight terminal. Police arrive to seize the
cargo, enforcers arrive to recover their money, and Stalkers converge on the sealed
containers.

Mara must move the captives through the terminal while deciding what to do with
the shipment. The climax combines vehicle escape, close-quarters encounters, and
stretches where remaining silent matters more than winning a fight.

The ending turns on two separate decisions:

- **Evidence:** Publish a corroborated account, surrender it under negotiated
  protections, or trade it for immediate escape.
- **Cargo:** Contain it at the terminal or allow it to leave with the convoy,
  shifting the danger elsewhere.

Mara can rescue her brother without exposing the network, or expose the network
while losing the safest evacuation route. The strongest outcome requires
preserving witnesses and building trust throughout the quest.

**Final objective:** Get the captives out before the terminal becomes a feeding
ground.

The invasion does not erase the turf war. It exposes how the feud, the enforcement
response, and the larger trafficking business each leave civilians trapped—and
gives Mara a concrete reason to cross all three systems: bring her brother home
with evidence strong enough to keep him from disappearing again.
